In short: we only collect the details you choose to send us through our enquiry forms, we use them to respond to you, and we never sell them. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time by writing to admin@athenadataflo.com.

Who we are

Athenadataflo (“we”, “us”) builds AI-powered analytics products, including Apollo Smart BI, and custom enterprise analytics applications. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through our website, athenadataflo.com. For any privacy question, contact us at admin@athenadataflo.com or +91 70324 49621.

Information we collect

We collect personal information only when you choose to send it to us:

  • Enquiry and demo request forms — your name, work email, company, role or team, area of interest and the message you write.
  • Email and phone — whatever you include when you contact us directly.

Our website does not ask you to create an account and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

We use the information you send us to:

  • reply to your enquiry or demo request;
  • arrange product demonstrations, early access or follow-up conversations you ask for;
  • keep a record of our correspondence with you.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Services we rely on

A small number of trusted providers help us run the website. They process information on our behalf or when you use their features:

  • Google Workspace (Google Sheets and Apps Script) — stores the enquiries submitted through our forms.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website and may log technical information such as your IP address for security and reliability.
  • Google Fonts — serves the fonts used on our pages.
  • YouTube — our product videos use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode, so YouTube does not set cookies until you press play.
  • Posit Connect Cloud — hosts the Apollo Smart BI application. Data you upload or analyse inside the app is processed by the app and its hosting provider, not through this website.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to respond to you and maintain a record of our business relationship, and generally no longer than 24 months after our last contact unless a longer period is required by law. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it, or withdraw consent you have given;
  • address a concern or grievance about how we handled it.

To make a request, email admin@athenadataflo.com. We will respond within a reasonable time, and in any case within the period the law requires.

Security

We use reputable providers and limit access to enquiry data to the people who need it to respond to you. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

Our website and products are intended for businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be highlighted on this page.

Contact us

Questions, requests or grievances about privacy can be sent to admin@athenadataflo.com or by phone on +91 70324 49621. You can also reach us through our LinkedIn page.
