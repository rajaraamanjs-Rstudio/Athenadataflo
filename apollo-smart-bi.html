Designed for business teams

Spend less time getting started. More time understanding the result.

Traditional BI workflows can involve multiple steps between having a question and producing an analysis. Apollo Smart BI is designed to make that first step more conversational and approachable.

  • Make data exploration more accessible to non-technical users.
  • Move from a natural-language question to a visual starting point.
  • Inspect and refine charts and tables as the analysis develops.
  • Use the resulting insights as inputs to business discussions.
